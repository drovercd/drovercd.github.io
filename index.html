DroverCD

Herd your agents to production.

DroverCD is an open source agentic release pipeline. A drover coordinates a swarm of AI agents that work in parallel to carry a change from idea to release.

  • Drover: the pipeline that plans the work, tracks it, and drives it toward release.
  • Swarm: parallel agents that pick up the pieces and do the work.
